The shift

The instinct is to ask what to try next.

Another feature. Another hire. Another process change. Another strategy conversation.

Sometimes that is exactly what is needed.

But when reasonable things keep failing to create meaningful progress, the more useful question is different.

Why didn’t the sensible things you already tried get you where you wanted to be?

That question usually reveals more than another brainstorm. It shows what the team has been assuming, what everyone has been optimizing for, and where the explanation no longer fits reality.

WHY me

Over the years, I worked with founders through some of their most difficult product and business decisions.

The most valuable part of my work wasn't coming up with new ideas. It was recognizing when sensible decisions were no longer leading to sensible outcomes, even though each individual decision still seemed perfectly reasonable.

someone who can see what has become invisible from the inside, before more time, money and effort are invested in the wrong direction.
